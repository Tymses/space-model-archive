ABOUT SMA

A clear index for space models.

Space Model Archive organises links to original 3D model sources. The goal is to make geometry easier to discover while keeping provenance, scale, dimensions and licensing transparent.

Models remain hosted by their original providers wherever possible. SMA does not include a slicer: users prepare and slice files in their own software. Print assessments will only appear when supported by actual test data.
